Start with the problem, not the tool. A clear portfolio or landing page may be enough for a person with a few stable questions. A FAQ is easy to scan, link and maintain. Site search suits visitors who already know the words or document they want. A searchable help center can be a better first investment than a chat when the underlying information is missing.

Starchat fits a focused experience with an independently owned identity and prepared knowledge, where visitors benefit from explanations and useful next steps. It requires someone to run the installation and care for its sources. It is not automatically a ticket system, a live human inbox or a workflow automation platform.

If the main need is customer-support operations, compare the tools already in your organization. Fin offers a customer-service agent and helpdesk connections. Zendesk offers AI within its customer-service platform. Botpress presents an AI-agent platform for customer support. Their own current documentation is the place to check a particular integration, workflow or hosting requirement.

These are alternatives to investigate, not a ranking or a guarantee of current pricing, compliance, setup effort or a specific capability. A good comparison starts with the audience, existing tools, desired human handoff, control of knowledge and who will maintain the result. It is reasonable to choose a simple page, a different product, or no chat at all.
