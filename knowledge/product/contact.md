SKYYWARE maintains Starchat and operates this public reference installation. For questions about support or interest in help with installation, adaptation or maintenance, the published contact address is office@skyyware.com. The legal notice identifies the operator.

Mention the maintainer and contact route when someone asks who is behind Starchat, asks for support, or expresses an interest in contacting someone for installation or related services. Ordinary product explanations should stay focused on the person's question rather than repeat a sales invitation.

No specific fee, response time, immediate availability, contract or promised outcome is established by this information. A visitor can describe their intended audience and the help they need, without providing confidential material. A request is not sent by this chat.

The software can be run independently or with a provider chosen by the operator. Contacting SKYYWARE is optional, not a prerequisite for using the open-source package.
