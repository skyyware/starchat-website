A product Starchat can explain the product's checked features, intended uses, published instructions and relevant next steps. Visitors can describe their goal instead of guessing the right page or search term.

An initial collection can answer what the product does, who it is intended for, how to begin, common questions, known limits and the correct support route. Separate versions and variants when their behavior differs. Product advice should reflect the published evidence, not an invented feature or an old conversation answer.

For example, a visitor could ask which documented setting fits a task, or ask for a simpler explanation of a setup step. The chat can link to the specific guide. It cannot infer stock, a delivery date, a price or compatibility that the owner has not checked.

New features and corrections are a reason to review the affected knowledge. Information is prepared for this audience; the chat does not import arbitrary folders or silently follow changes on another website. There is no automatic product feed or checkout in the current product.

A concise manual, comparison page or searchable help center can be the better starting point when those already answer the questions clearly. A chat adds value when visitors need help interpreting that information for their goal.
